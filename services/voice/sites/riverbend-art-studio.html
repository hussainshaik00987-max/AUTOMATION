About

Riverbend Art Studio

We're a wedding photography studio based in Sacramento, working with couples who'd rather be photographed than posed.

Most of our work is documentary: we stay out of the way, watch what's happening, and shoot it as it comes. Where a portrait needs directing we'll direct it, then get out of the frame again.

Before you get in touch

How far in advance should we book?
Peak wedding dates in Sacramento go early — the sooner you check, the better. Send us your dates and we'll tell you honestly whether we're free.
Do you travel outside Sacramento?
Yes. Travel and stay are quoted separately and agreed upfront, with no surprises added later.
When do we get our photos?
You'll get a set of previews within days of the wedding, and the full edited gallery after that. We confirm exact timelines in writing when you book.

Please do. A shortlist of family groupings and must-have moments makes the day run faster for everyone.
