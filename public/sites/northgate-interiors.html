Process

How a project runs

No mystery, no surprises — here is exactly what happens from your first message.

Consultation

A site visit and a conversation about how you use the space, and the budget you're working to.

Concept & layout

Space plans, mood direction and an indicative budget, presented for your feedback.

Design development

3D views, material samples and detailed drawings — signed off before procurement.

Execution

Contractors mobilised, materials scheduled, site supervised on a fixed visit cadence.

Handover

Snagging closed out, warranties compiled, and a walkthrough of how everything works.

Questions

Before you get in touch

What does a project typically cost?
It depends entirely on area, scope and material spec. After a site visit we give you an indicative range before you commit to anything.
Do you handle execution or only design?
Both. You can take the drawings and appoint your own contractor, or we deliver turnkey with one point of accountability.
How long does a project take?
Design is usually a few weeks; site work depends on scope and approvals. You'll get a phased timeline with the proposal.
Do you work outside Atlanta?
Yes, subject to site visit frequency. We'll be upfront about what supervision looks like for a remote site.
